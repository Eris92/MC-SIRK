# SIRK Management Platform 1.7.19

- Scopes the two legacy Quick lifecycle observers to `#SirkDesktopCommandsPanel` instead of the whole MeshCentral document.
- Prevents Quick from reacting to DOM mutations produced by My Scripts, Approval Center and the Commands device page.
- Reattaches scoped observers when the Quick panel is recreated, without requiring a page refresh.
- Keeps observers unrelated to Quick on their original targets.
- Synchronizes the selected device tab heading to `Commands - <PC>` while preserving MeshCentral's native device-name suffix.
- Adds a browser-hook regression test for production `pluginadmin.ashx?...asset=*.js&v=...` URLs, observer isolation and the Commands heading.
